WHO Collaborating Centre

Learn the methods MAP uses to inform global malaria policy.

MAP is a designated World Health Organization Collaborating Centre in Geospatial Disease Modelling. Through this partnership, we provide modelling and analysis directly to the WHO Global Malaria Programme — with estimates cited in the World Malaria Report and the Global Burden of Disease study.

This training is taught by the same scientists producing that work. You'll learn the exact methods MAP uses to inform global and national malaria policy.

What You'll Gain

Skills that outlast the training

Expert-Led Training

Hands-on training in advanced statistical models, taught by leading experts in geospatial modelling and public health.

Modern Methods

Current geospatial modelling methods — including Bayesian approaches with INLA and INLAbru — from practitioners actively using them in malaria research.

Real-World Data

Work with the same spatial and real-world datasets MAP uses to produce WHO's global malaria estimates.

Regional Network

Join a growing regional network of geospatial modellers across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Certification

Career Impact
